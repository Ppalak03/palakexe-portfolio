PROJECT_02 · CONCEPT & DESIGN

InnerInk — Your Thoughts. Your Space. Your Privacy.

InnerInk is a privacy-focused digital journaling concept for personal writing and reflection. Its design priorities include protecting journal content, balancing confidentiality with recovery options, minimizing sensitive metadata, and preferring on-device processing for future reflections. Architecture and offline-writing decisions are still being explored.
